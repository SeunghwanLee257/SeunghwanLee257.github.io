Blockchain is the most natural long-term product surface for FHE16 because encrypted state, public verification, and selective disclosure need a fast confidential computation engine.

## Product Problem

Most blockchain applications expose state too early:

- Bids.
- Scores.
- Balances.
- Identity or eligibility attributes.
- Strategy parameters.

FHE16 can support workflows where values remain encrypted while specific computations are still possible.

## FHE16 Role

- Encrypt user or protocol inputs.
- Compute comparisons or selections over encrypted values.
- Return encrypted outputs or selectively disclosed results.
- Support deterministic, auditable workflows where applicable.

## API Methods Needed

- `context_create`
- `encrypt_u16`
- `lt`
- `eq`
- `max`
- `select`
- `serialize_ciphertext`
- `deserialize_ciphertext`
- `decrypt_u16` or threshold disclosure wrapper

## Documentation Boundary

Blockchain pages should explain trust models carefully. A chain integration may require additional proof, verification, threshold, or oracle layers beyond the core FHE16 local API.
