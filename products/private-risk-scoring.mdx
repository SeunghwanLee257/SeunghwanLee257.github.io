Private risk scoring is the enterprise product path: compute risk signals without moving or exposing raw records.

## Product Problem

Institutions often need to compare sensitive values across boundaries:

- Insurance claims.
- Identity attributes.
- Account or transaction patterns.
- Fraud signals.
- Eligibility checks.

The raw data cannot always be shared, copied, or centralized.

## FHE16 Role

FHE16 can help expose limited computation over private values:

- Equality checks.
- Range checks.
- Max or threshold selection.
- Rule-based score components.

## API Methods Needed

- `encrypt_u16`
- `encrypt_bool`
- `eq`
- `lt`
- `add`
- `select`
- `decrypt_bool`
- `decrypt_u16`

## Multi-Party Direction

For cross-institution workflows, this product track will eventually connect to SSFHE or threshold disclosure documentation. Keep the single-party FHE16 API and multi-party policy layer separate.
