## Caller

Unsigned modulo by powers of two.

## Raw Symbols

- `FHE16_MOD_POW2`

## Circuit Path

Copies lower k encrypted bits.

## Output

Unsigned remainder ciphertext.

## Ownership

Returned ciphertext is caller-owned.

## C Example

```c
int32_t* out = FHE16_MOD_POW2(a, 3);
FHE16_Free(out);
```
