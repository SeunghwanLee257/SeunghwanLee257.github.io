## Caller

Signed modulo by powers of two.

## Raw Symbols

- `FHE16_SMOD_POW2`

## Circuit Path

Signed pow2 division, shift back, then FHE16_SUB.

## Output

Signed remainder ciphertext.

## Ownership

Returned ciphertext is caller-owned.

## C Example

```c
int32_t* out = FHE16_SMOD_POW2(a, 3);
FHE16_Free(out);
```
