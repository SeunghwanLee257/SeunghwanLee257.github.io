## Caller

Signed division by powers of two with C-style rounding.

## Raw Symbols

- `FHE16_SDIV_POW2`

## Circuit Path

Sign-derived bias plus FHE16_ADD, then shift toward zero.

## Output

Signed quotient ciphertext.

## Ownership

Returned ciphertext is caller-owned.

## C Example

```c
int32_t* out = FHE16_SDIV_POW2(a, 3);
FHE16_Free(out);
```
