## Caller

Signed division, signed modulo, scoring, and range normalization.

## Raw Symbols

- `FHE16_ABS`

## Circuit Path

PrefixABS path over encrypted sign and magnitude bits.

## Output

n-bit absolute-value ciphertext.

## Ownership

Returned ciphertext is caller-owned.

## C Example

```c
int32_t* out = FHE16_ABS(a);
FHE16_Free(out);
```
