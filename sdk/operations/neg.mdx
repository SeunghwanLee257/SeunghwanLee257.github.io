## Caller

Signed arithmetic helpers, division, modulo, and product correction paths.

## Raw Symbols

## Circuit Path

## Output

n-bit integer ciphertext.

## Ownership

Returned ciphertext is caller-owned.

## C Example

```c
int32_t* out = FHE16_NEG(a);
FHE16_Free(out);
```
