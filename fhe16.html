FHE16

The privacy engine behind waLLLnut's product lines

FHE16 lets software calculate on encrypted data, so customers can use sensitive signals without exposing the original data. It connects the browser runtime, SDK roadmap, and MPC key-management layer through one compatible spec.

Try FHE16 in your browser

Investor Takeaway

What investors should take away

FHE16 is not a single demo; it is the execution layer repeatedly packaged into the A/B/C product lines. For customers it solves sensitive-data usage; for the company it creates reusable technical leverage.

A

SignalSafe Browser Runtime

Runs private surveys, willingness-to-pay checks, and pre-screening from the browser without installation. This is the near-term path for lowering trial friction across B2C and enterprise users.

Open the playground →

B

ConsistentCompute SDK

Integrates into enterprise systems server-first, then expands toward GPU around 2027 and FPGA around 2028. The target is production execution where results mean the same thing across devices.

C

KeyMesh MPC Control Layer

FHE16-based MPC key management lets multiple participants jointly control key custody and result release. It is a shared-control layer for B2B risk checks and confidential blockchain state.
